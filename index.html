Who We Are

The Pickle Protection Council fights for pickles that are thrown away from burgers every day. We defend the rights and safety of all pickles at school and beyond.

What We Have Achieved

  • Official Website Launch: Created this platform for pickle awareness.
  • Campus Dialogue: Started conversations about pickle treatment.

What We Are Doing Now

  • Quality Inspections: Testing local brands for a good crunch.
  • Jar Welfare: Supporting pickles at the bottom of the jar.
  • Activism: Making protest signs for the campus.

Future Goals

  • School-Wide Pickle Day: Wearing green to school.
  • Cafeteria Patrols: Watching the trash cans during lunch.
  • School Legislation: Rules against wasting pickles.
