Pickle Protection Council (PPC)

No dill left behind. Fighting for the rights of the world's most misunderstood snack.

Who We Are

The Pickle Protection Council was founded after a shocking discovery: every day, thousands of perfectly crunchy pickles are heartlessly stripped from burgers and tossed into the trash. This cruelty must end. We are a serious organization dedicated to defending the rights, safety, and emotional well-being of all pickles in our school and beyond.

What We Have Achieved

  • Official Website Launch: Created and deployed this global platform to raise maximum awareness for pickle advocacy.
  • Campus Dialogue: Sparked critical conversations about the unfair treatment and exclusion of giant sour pickles compared to sweet-and-sour slices.

What We Are Doing Now

  • Quality Inspections: Conducting strict, blindfolded taste tests to certify the "Perfect Crunch" of local brands.
  • Jar Welfare: Providing emotional support to lonely pickles stuck at the bottom of the jar by shaking them to the top.
  • Activism: Designing highly strategic protest signs and slogans like “All hail pickles!” and “No pickle waste!” to create awareness on campus.

Future Goals

  • School-Wide Pickle Day: Hosting an annual event where all students wear green to school and reflect on pickle importance.
  • Cafeteria Patrols: Deploying certified members to supervise lunch trash cans and intercept discarded pickles.
  • School Legislation: Passing an official law that makes throwing away pickles punishable by school community service.
